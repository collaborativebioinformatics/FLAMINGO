The shared exposure

Every dataset starts here, no matter what outcome comes next: independent SNP instruments plus one confounder U combine into a single exposure X. h2_x sets how much of X the instruments explain; gamma_x sets how much U leaks in.

X = Σ βjGj + γxU + ex  ·  Var(X)=1

Any causal shape

Continuous default

The exposure's effect plus confounding and Gaussian noise — the textbook MR target for 2SLS/IVW-style estimators.

Y = f(X) + γyU + ey

Binary case / control

The same signal as a liability, collapsed to 0/1 by a threshold or a logistic link — prevalence is a dial, not an accident.

Y = 1{liability > cutoff}

Survival time-to-event

Cox proportional hazards over a Weibull baseline, with censoring — theta becomes a log hazard ratio instead of a mean shift.

h(t) = h₀(t)·exp(θX + γyU)

Federated, not identical

Ten sites share one causal curve — the biology — while population size, instrument strength, and confounding vary independently per site, like real biobanks across countries.

Pluggable by design

The site orchestrator never touches outcome logic — it just calls whatever the registry hands it. New outcome type, zero shared-file edits.
